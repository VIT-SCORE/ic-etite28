Registrations

Registration categories, fees and the steps to secure your place at ic-ETITE'26.

Fees

Registration categories

Choose the category that applies to you. At least one author per accepted paper must complete registration.

CategoryIndian delegatesForeign delegates

Fees are indicative and cover conference access, proceedings and delegate kit. Confirm current fees on the official registration portal before paying.

How to register

Registration steps

  1. Get your paper accepted

    Registration for authors opens once a paper has cleared review and a camera-ready version is ready.

  2. Choose your portal

    Indian authors and participants register through one portal; foreign authors and participants use a separate international registration portal.

  3. Complete payment

    Pay the applicable fee online. A confirmation receipt is issued immediately after successful payment.

  4. Receive confirmation

    A registration confirmation and delegate ID are sent by email, required for camera-ready upload and on-site check-in.

Register — Indian delegates Register — Foreign delegates
Cancellations & refunds: Registration fees are generally non-refundable once camera-ready papers have been accepted for proceedings. For exceptional circumstances, contact the organising committee directly before the registration deadline.
