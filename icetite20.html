Highlights of ic-ETITE'20

A look back at the first International Conference on Emerging Trends in Information Technology and Engineering.

Previous edition

24–25 February 2020, VIT Vellore

The first edition of the conference was technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society Madras Chapter and the IEEE Communications Society Madras Chapter, with support from the ACM Madras Chapter. All presented papers were published in IEEE Xplore.
