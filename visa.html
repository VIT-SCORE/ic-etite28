Visa assistance

Guidance for international delegates who need a conference invitation letter or visa support to attend ic-ETITE'26.

For international delegates

Getting your invitation letter

Foreign nationals attending ic-ETITE'26 in person typically require a conference (business) visa to enter India. The steps below outline how to request supporting documentation from the organising committee.

  1. Confirm your registration

    Invitation letters are issued only after your paper is accepted and your conference registration is complete.

  2. Request an invitation letter

    Email the organising committee with your full name (as on passport), nationality, passport number and paper ID.

  3. Receive your letter

    A signed invitation letter on VIT letterhead is emailed to you, referencing the conference dates and your accepted paper.

  4. Apply at your nearest Indian mission

    Submit the invitation letter along with your visa application at the nearest Indian embassy, high commission or consulate.

Please note: Visa rules and processing times vary by country and change from time to time. This page is general guidance only — always confirm current requirements with your nearest Indian embassy, high commission or consulate well ahead of travel.

Need an invitation letter?

Write to the organising committee with your registration details and we'll email your invitation letter.
