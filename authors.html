Submission process

How to submit your paper

Follow these steps in order. All submissions are handled through the Microsoft CMT portal — email submissions are not accepted.

  1. Prepare your manuscript

    Format your paper using the IEEE ic-ETITE'26 conference template. Manuscripts must not contain embedded links, scanned images, or headers and footers.

  2. Check originality

    Ensure the work is original and unpublished. Every submission is automatically screened for plagiarism on receipt.

  3. Submit via CMT

    Create an account and upload your paper through the Microsoft CMT submission portal linked below.

  4. Track review status

    Reviewers assess submissions for technical quality, originality and relevance to the conference tracks. Decisions are communicated through CMT.

  5. Submit the camera-ready version

    Accepted authors revise their paper per reviewer feedback and upload the final camera-ready copy ahead of the deadline.

  6. Register & present

    At least one author per accepted paper must register and present the work at the conference for it to be included in proceedings.
