Submission process

How to submit your paper

Submit original, unpublished papers through EasyChair. Email submissions are not accepted.

  1. Prepare your manuscript

    Format your paper using the IEEE ic-ETITE'28 conference template. Manuscripts must not contain embedded links, scanned images, or headers and footers.

    Browse Paper Presentation Tracks

  2. Check originality

    Ensure the work is original and unpublished. Every submission is automatically screened for plagiarism on receipt.

  3. Submit via EasyChair

    Use the official EasyChair conference portal to submit your paper.

  4. Track review status

    Reviewers assess submissions for technical quality, originality and relevance to the conference tracks. Review decisions are communicated through the conference's EasyChair portal.

  5. Submit the camera-ready version

    Accepted authors revise their paper per reviewer feedback and upload the final camera-ready copy ahead of the deadline.

  6. Register & present

    At least one author per accepted paper must register and present the work at the conference for it to be included in proceedings.
