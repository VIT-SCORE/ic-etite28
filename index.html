About the conference

Why ic-ETITE exists

The conference exists to strengthen research across Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Communication Engineering and Electronics, giving researchers, academicians, engineers, industry professionals and students a shared international platform.

Beyond publication, the goal is connection: helping delegates build research and business relationships that carry into future collaborations across their careers. Original, unpublished work is welcomed from every relevant subject area.

Papers accepted and presented are taken forward for indexing in IEEE Xplore, extending each author's work to a global technical audience.

Conference theme

Upgrading research in IT and engineering

ic-ETITE places a deliberate focus on advancing Information Technology and Engineering research, offering a worldwide stage for innovators to present work that moves the field forward.

The underlying mission is to promote development that is both rigorous and connected — helping researchers find collaborators beyond their own institutions and borders, and giving early findings a clear path toward real academic impact.

The host institution

About VIT Vellore

ic-ETITE is hosted at Vellore Institute of Technology, home to the School of Computer Science Engineering and Information Systems.

Vellore Institute of Technology was founded in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College and was granted university status in 2001. It is led by founder-chancellor Dr. G. Viswanathan and today draws students and faculty from around the world.

The institute is built around a consistent focus on improving higher-education quality, with a strong support structure that carries students through their time on campus, guided by its mission of transforming lives through education and research.

SCORE — formerly SITE — runs B.Tech (IT), M.Tech (Software Engineering), MCA, BCA, B.Sc (Computer Science), M.Tech by Research and Ph.D programmes in Information Technology and Engineering.

The school has more than 5,900 students and 188 faculty members, backed by state-of-the-art labs, strong industry linkages, and a long track record of hosting seminars, workshops and conferences in emerging technical areas.

The IEEE Information Theory Society student chapter at VIT is a newer chapter focused on exploring current advances in information theory and applying them to real problems.

It runs technical events and workshops and partners with collaborators on applied projects, aiming to help students build confidence tackling open technical challenges.

Manuscript submission

Papers are submitted through the conference's Microsoft CMT portal and go through a plagiarism check on receipt. Manuscripts should be free of embedded links, scanned images, headers and footers — submissions by email are not accepted.
